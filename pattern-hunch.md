# Pattern Hunch

> **I have a hunch that** people grow fastest when I hand the work back
> to them as a question, and that finishing it myself, however
> well-meant, costs them the growth,
>
> **but I've only seen it** four times, in three contexts.

**The evidence**

| Story | Context | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| 1. "Don't tell me it's what I expected" | Work | Refusing the easy answer got her to the sharper question |
| 2. "Let people fail to succeed" | Work | Shielding someone from the real job kept them from growing |
| 3. "Stealing the stretch work" | Volunteer org | Finishing it myself took the stretch from someone else |
| 4. "My sister picks three" | Family | Asking while she built moved the method without me |

**Honest limits**

- Two of the three contexts have one story each.
- Stories 1 and 2 are the same person, in the same job.
- Story 5 is a counter-example I can't yet explain: there I said "not
  yet" instead of handing the work back.

**Status:** a hunch, not a pattern. It becomes a pattern when new
stories from other contexts keep confirming it. Until then: not yet.
Keep observing.
