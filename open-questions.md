## Questions I can't answer yet

- **When does asking a question stop being coaching and start being
  withholding?** Story 5: I held her back instead of handing the work
  over. I still can't say whether that was right.
- **Am I doing the talking when I think I'm handing the work back?**
  Someone I was handing meetings to told me he still wasn't leading
  them, because I was doing most of the talking. It is the question I'd
  least like anyone to ask me out loud.
- **Does anything I automate for someone else take back the thinking I
  meant to hand them?** I built a daily brief so he could prepare for
  meetings. It helped, but I'm not sure it's the same as him doing the
  preparing.
